# Demo video pitch

Target length: about 3 minutes.

## Opening

"This is QwenVideo, a story-to-video pipeline for the Backblaze Generative Media Hackathon. It turns a prompt into a structured production workflow, generates scenes with Genblaze-powered providers, and stores every generated asset and metadata record in Backblaze B2."

## What to show

1. Open the dashboard.
2. Create a new story from a short prompt.
3. Open the story workspace and show the assistant/chat refinement flow.
4. Approve the outline.
5. Launch generation.
6. Show the generated scene or job status.
7. Open the library or export/share screen to show the stored outputs.
8. Mention that video files, exit frames, manifests, and provenance data are stored in Backblaze B2.

## Core talking points

- Genblaze is the orchestration layer for the media pipeline.
- Backblaze B2 is the durable storage layer for generated assets.
- The app tracks asset lineage and relationships, so scenes and references stay connected.
- The workflow supports continuity, refinement, and regeneration instead of a one-shot prompt.
- The frontend is designed for creators: create, refine, approve, generate, export, share.

## Short ending

"The main idea is simple: prompt to pipeline to durable storage. QwenVideo shows how AI media can be generated reliably, tracked clearly, and stored safely on Backblaze B2."
